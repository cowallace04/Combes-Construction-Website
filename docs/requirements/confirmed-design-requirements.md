# Confirmed Design Requirements

## Visual Direction

- Entire site will use a dark visual theme.
- Use multiple coordinated blue shades.
- Use mature sans-serif typography.
- Use horizontal desktop navigation.
- Use large construction photography.
- Use short, muted, repeating videos on the homepage.
- Use subtle tile and section movement during scrolling.
- Use a white or monochrome logo over dark backgrounds and photography.

## Homepage

- Begin with a video hero.
- Place the primary Projects link prominently.
- Follow the hero with a project collage.
- Use Newkirk Novak as the primary structural reference for the homepage.
- Include featured projects, company introduction, services, process, safety, team, careers and contact sections.

## Projects

- Use a tile-based project archive.
- Support Current and Completed project statuses.
- Include project name, location, owner, architect, timeline and description.
- Support project image galleries.
- Use subtle hover overlays and image movement.

## Team

- Include every team member.
- Approximately 13 team members are expected.
- Use professional headshots.
- Show name, position and email.
- Include years with Combes.
- Include total years in the construction industry.
- Include education within the biography.
- Include life outside work.
- Do not include phone numbers.
- Do not include LinkedIn profiles.
- Do not include certifications.

## Contact and Social Media

- Place address, phone and general email on the Contact page.
- Repeat address, phone and general email in the footer.
- Include LinkedIn and Facebook.
- Do not include Instagram.

## Navigation

- Use horizontal navigation on desktop.
- Use dropdowns for related child pages.
- Use a collapsed navigation menu on mobile.
